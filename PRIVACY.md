# BeerCHILLER — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 4 August 2026_

## Summary

**BeerCHILLER collects no data. It has no network code.**

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no tracking
and no server of any kind. Nothing you enter leaves your device, because there is
nothing in the app that could send it anywhere.

## What the app stores, and where

BeerCHILLER saves only what it needs to keep working between launches:

| Stored | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Your inputs (container, volume, position, start/target/appliance temperature) | So the app opens with the settings you last used |
| Your appearance choices (visual style, light/dark, temperature unit) | So the app looks the way you left it |
| The running timer (start time, end time, the inputs it was started with) | So a running cool-down survives closing the app or restarting the phone |

All of it is stored **on your device**, in an App Group shared between the
BeerCHILLER app, its widget and its Apple Watch app. That sharing is local: it is
how the widget can show your countdown and how the watch can stop a run. Nothing
is uploaded, synchronised to a server, or shared with us or anyone else.

Deleting the app removes all of it.

## Permissions

* **Notifications** — asked for only when you start a timer, so the app can tell
  you the beer is ready while it is closed. Declining leaves everything else
  working; you simply have to open the app to see the countdown.

The app asks for nothing else. It does not request location, contacts, photos,
microphone, camera, health data, or Bluetooth.

## Apple

If you install BeerCHILLER from the App Store, Apple processes the download and
any diagnostics you have chosen to share with Apple under
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We receive no
personal data from Apple — only the anonymous, aggregated sales and crash
statistics that App Store Connect shows every developer.

## Children

BeerCHILLER is a utility for chilling beverages and is not directed at children.
It collects no data from anyone, of any age.
